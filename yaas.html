YaaS · Yield as a Service

Yield as a Service: we do not sell substrate as a consumable, but package material, protocol, equipment and intelligence into a deliverable production capability. What we deliver is the full set of conditions for stable operation, not a truckload of wool.

Slope upwards: the higher the layer, the closer to an outcome and the further from a material.

02 / Services

Services layered on demonstration sites

01

On-site training

Sowing, potting, nutrient preparation, routine inspection — worked through clause by clause.

02

Resident support

On site during key nodes of the first season for temperature and humidity swings and nutrient imbalance.

03

Remote diagnosis

Parameter review during operation: EC/pH drift, root-zone water anomalies, early disease screening.
