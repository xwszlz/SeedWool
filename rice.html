Rice Seedling Substrate

Rockwool boards, mats and plugs for centralised nursery of machine-transplanted rice. Inorganic fibre replaces topsoil extraction; seedling vigour is uniform and missed hills decline. The achievement 'Rockwool seedling technology for machine-transplanted rice' is registered at both provincial and national level, with the company as first completion unit.

30% of 2029 target mixDual registrationProtocol due 2027
01 / Overview

Replacing topsoil extraction with an industrial substrate

Conventional nurseries extract large volumes of topsoil, damaging the cultivated layer, while unstable soil sources cause uneven emergence. Rockwool boards use inorganic fibre as the base, combined with a dedicated nutrient solution and temperature-humidity management, so every tray is consistent and every batch traceable.
