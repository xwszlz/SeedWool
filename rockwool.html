02 / Accounting note

Why this line is presented separately

Agricultural rockwool and building thermal insulation rockwool belong to different standard systems. GB/T 11835, GB/T 19686 and GB/T 25975 are all building insulation standards and cannot serve as acceptance criteria for propagation or growing media; conversely, agri-media protocols do not apply to building insulation acceptance. Presenting the two on one chart invites legitimate doubt about data discipline.

No capacity or revenue targets are stated here. The 140,000 t / CNY 2bn figure belongs to the agri-media plan for 2029 and does not relate to this line.
