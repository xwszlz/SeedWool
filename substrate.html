Controlled-Environment Growing Media

Rockwool slabs and raised-trough media for tomato, pepper, leafy greens and strawberry. Inorganic fibre replaces soil, turning water and nutrient supply, root-zone air and disease risk into adjustable parameters. Delivered with a 14-chapter growing-media protocol and a 16-chapter strawberry protocol.

02 / Why rockwool instead of soil

Why rockwool instead of soil

CriterionSoilCoir / peatRockwool
Root-zone water controlLimited by soil textureGood, prone to compactionPrecisely set by irrigation strategy
Soil-borne diseaseRequires rotation or fumigationLow, but mould riskSterile inorganic substrate
Continuous-cropping fatiguePronouncedMilderReplaced each cycle
Batch consistencyLowMedium (origin dependent)Industrial, traceable batches
End-of-life—Organic decompositionInorganic fibre, recyclable

The value is not in replacing soil as such, but in converting uncontrollable soil variables into measurable, reproducible industrial parameters.
