Global Business

Products ship to more than sixty countries and regions. We therefore manage country admission in three tiers — A, B and C — and never treat an insulation approval as a media approval.

02 / Admission notes (essential for export buyers)

Admission notes (essential for export buyers)

  • Most countries require separate quarantine admission for plant growing media, with composition, sterilisation process and origin documentation.
  • CE/EN or other certification for building insulation rockwool cannot be used for media admission.
  • Quarantine requirements change; confirm current rules for the destination before quotation — we provide a status statement.
  • Sample dispatch is subject to the same restrictions; some countries require an import permit in advance.
