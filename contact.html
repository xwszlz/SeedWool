02 / How to get a useful answer

How to get a useful answer

  • State the crop and method
    e.g. winter tomato on rockwool slabs in raised troughs
  • Give area or annual volume
    e.g. 50 mu, about 12,000 slabs in year one
  • Mention region and climate
    e.g. Hengshui, Hebei; winter minimum -12°C
  • Describe existing problems
    e.g. three consecutive seasons, heavy root-knot nematode
